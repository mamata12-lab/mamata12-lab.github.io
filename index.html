What's Behind the Looking Glass?

The goal of Behind the Looking Glass is to use my personal reef aquariums to show visitors the complexity behind maintaining a healthy saltwater ecosystem.

The website provides practical and educational information to help visitors better understand reef keeping and the commitment involved.
